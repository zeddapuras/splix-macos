SpliX for macOS

Open source CUPS driver for SPL (QPDL) laser printers by Samsung, Xerox, Dell, HP, Lexmark and Toshiba, built natively for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or later).

It is a replacement for vendor drivers that no longer work on recent macOS versions (e.g. Samsung UPD failing with "Filter failed" on Apple Silicon).

Installs:

  • the rastertoqpdl filter in /Library/Printers/SpliX/Filters
  • 104 printer descriptions (PPD) in /Library/Printers/PPDs, shown as "… (SpliX)"

Based on OpenPrinting SpliX (GPL v2). Unofficial build, not affiliated with any printer manufacturer.
