Add your printer

  1. Open System Settings → Printers & Scanners → Add Printer…
  2. Select your printer.
  3. Under Use, choose Select Software… and pick your model ending with (SpliX).
  4. Click Add.
  5. Optional: to always use it with Cmd+P, on the same page set Default printer to your printer (instead of "Last Printer Used").

In the print dialog always pick the (SpliX) printer, not the one listed under "Nearby Printers".

If you already have a queue for this printer using the vendor driver, remove it first.

To uninstall: sudo /Library/Printers/SpliX/uninstall.sh
